`mcu.js` is a bundled build of [@material/material-color-utilities](https://github.com/material-foundation/material-color-utilities)
(Apache License 2.0, © Google LLC), used to turn an image or color into a Material palette, the same algorithm matugen uses.
Source of the bundle: `tools/mcu-entry.js` (built with `esbuild --bundle --format=iife --global-name=MCU --minify`).
