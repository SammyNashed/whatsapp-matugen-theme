# Privacy Policy

**Wallpaper Theme for WhatsApp Web** (also published as *WhatsApp Matugen Theme*) changes how WhatsApp Web looks. It collects nothing.

## What the extension does
- It runs only on `https://web.whatsapp.com/` and changes colors, the chat background and panel styling.
- It **does not read, copy, store or transmit** your messages, contacts, photos, or any other WhatsApp content.
- It makes **no network requests to any server on the internet** and contains no analytics, tracking or advertising.

## What it stores
- The wallpaper image and color palette you choose are saved **only in your browser** (`chrome.storage.local`) so the theme can be applied. They never leave your device. Removing the extension deletes them.

## The optional desktop helper
- On Linux, an optional helper program can run on your own computer and answers only to `127.0.0.1:8765` (your own machine), so the extension can read your current wallpaper and colors. Nothing is sent over the internet.

## Permissions
- `storage` / `unlimitedStorage`: save your chosen wallpaper and palette locally.
- `alarms`: check for a wallpaper change every 30 seconds (helper mode).
- Access to `web.whatsapp.com`: to apply the theme there.
- Access to `127.0.0.1:8765`: to talk to the optional local helper.
